      Weekly Runs

      Three runs, every week, every pace.

      Trail Tuesday
      Tuesdays • 6:00 PM
      Lake McMurtry East Pavilion
      Stillwater, OK

      All paces welcome — hikers, walkers, and runners with 1 mile, 3.5 mile, 7 mile, or out-and-back options. Meet at the East Pavilion. Lake McMurtry charges a $6 day pass if you don't have an annual pass. In the darker months, bring a headlamp.

      Wednesday Pub Run
      Wednesdays • 6:30 PM
      Stonecloud Brewing
      Stillwater, OK

      A social road run from Stonecloud Brewing at 6:30pm — run or walk whatever distance you're feeling, then stick around for drinks and conversation. Every pace welcome, come see us!

      Aspen Run
      Saturdays • 7:00 AM
      Aspen Coffee Co.
      Stillwater, OK

      The Saturday morning staple. Meet at Aspen Coffee downtown for anything from an easy few miles to a long run, then stick around for coffee with the crew.
